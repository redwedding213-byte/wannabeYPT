What this is

wannabeYPT is a free, unofficial web client for YPT (Yeolpumta), built for students who want to use their YPT account in a desktop browser. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to YPT or TGCLab, and it is not a replacement for the official app.

Your YPT account

You sign in with your own YPT account. Your account, its data, and your use of YPT's service remain governed by YPT's own terms and policies. Keep your credentials to yourself — never share your password with anyone, including through the feedback form.

Acceptable use

  • Use the app only with your own account.
  • Don't attempt to disrupt the service, overload YPT's servers, or misuse group and ranking features.
  • Don't use the app to record study time you didn't actually study — it defeats the point for everyone in your groups.
