Privacy Policy

wannabeYPT — https://wannabeypt.vercel.app · Last updated: 21 October 2026

wannabeYPT is an unofficial web client for YPT (Yeolpumta). It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to YPT or TGCLab. This page explains, in plain language, what happens to your information when you use it.

We run no server of our own

wannabeYPT is a static web app. There is no wannabeYPT backend and no database where your account or study data is stored by us. The app in your browser talks directly to YPT's own servers, the same way the official app does.

Signing in

  • Email sign-in: your YPT email and password are sent from your browser directly to YPT's servers. They are never sent to, or stored by, us.
  • Google sign-in: Google verifies your identity and shares your basic profile (name, email address, profile picture) with the sign-in helper, which exchanges it for a YPT session. We do not receive or store your Google password.
  • Your YPT session token is stored only in your own browser's local storage, so you stay signed in. Signing out removes it. Anyone with access to your browser profile can access the session, just like any signed-in website.

Your study data

Your timers, study history, groups and tasks live on YPT's servers under your own YPT account and are governed by YPT's own policies. wannabeYPT only displays and starts/stops timers on your behalf.

Feedback

If you use the in-app feedback form, your message and your YPT nickname are emailed to the site owner through Web3Forms so bugs and requests can be acted on. Don't include passwords or private information in feedback.

Analytics

The site may use privacy-respecting, aggregate visit statistics (page views) that do not identify individual users.

Contact

Questions about this policy: use the feedback form inside the app (tap the “?” button → Send feedback).
